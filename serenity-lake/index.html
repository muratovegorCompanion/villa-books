House rules

It's a quiet residential area: no parties, events or loud music. Smoking only outside, never in the houses. Breakages and missing items are charged at replacement cost. Thai law requires us to register every guest (form TM30), so please send copies of all passports before you arrive.

Getting around

There's no Grab or Bolt on Koh Phangan, and taxis are expensive. Most guests rent a scooter or a car. The villa is about five minutes from Thong Sala, the island's main town and ferry pier.

Beaches

Approximate times from the villa by scooter or car. The south coast near Thong Sala is shallow; the clear water and sunsets are on the west coast, 15–25 minutes away.

  • ~10min

    Ban Tai & Ban Khai south coast

    The nearest beaches: a long, quiet stretch with coconut palms and a few beach bars. Very shallow — at low tide you can't really swim, so go at high tide or come for a walk and dinner at Fisherman's. Map

  • ~20min

    Haad Yao our pick

    A kilometre of sand on the west coast, a reef for snorkelling, restaurants and bars along the beach and a proper sunset every evening. Map

  • ~25min

    Haad Salad west coast

    Next to Haad Yao but smaller and quieter: fine white sand, clear water and coral a few metres off the beach. Calm and easy with kids. Map

  • ~20min

    Secret Beach · Haad Son sunset

    A small west-facing cove between Haad Yao and Zen Beach. Short, steep road down; you walk through Haad Son Resort. Drinks at Koh Raham on the rocks. It fills up — come early. Map

  • ~20min

    Zen Beach · Sri Thanu Friday sunset

    Every Friday people gather on the sand for a drum jam at sunset — drums, guitars, dancing. Come by 17:30 for a spot and parking. Shallow water; watch for rocks and sea urchins. Map

  • ~25min

    Haad Rin south-east

    Long, soft white sand and clear water, with more cafés and shops than anywhere else. Busy around the Full Moon Party. Watch for rip currents on Haad Rin Nok. Map

Shopping

Everything is in Thong Sala, about five minutes away: Makro (wholesale, big packs), Lotus's and Big C (around 8:00–22:00) for groceries and home things, and a 7‑Eleven with an ATM on almost every corner. Pharmacies and a Watsons are in the centre of Thong Sala, usually open 8:00–22:00 — 24‑hour pharmacies are rare.

Views & waterfalls

  • ~10min

    Wat Khao Tham above Ban Tai

    A hillside monastery with a view over the south coast to Koh Samui — lovely at sunset. Free; short steep walk from the parking. It's a working temple: cover shoulders and knees, shoes off inside. Map

  • 15–20min

    Phaeng Waterfall & Domsila viewpoint

    A national-park walk inland from Thong Sala: the waterfall, then 10–15 minutes uphill to the viewpoint. About 100 ฿, cash. Best after rain (October–December); weak in the dry season. Map

  • ~15min

    Amsterdam Bar sunset drinks

    On the hill on the west side, with a pool and an open view of the sunset. Open from noon; packed around Full Moon. Map

  • 2–3hours

Further out

Day trips on the island

  • 35–45min

    Thong Nai Pan Noi & Yai north-east

    Two of the most beautiful bays on the island. The road is paved but steep with tight bends — go by car if you're not confident on a scooter, and stop at the viewpoint on the way down. Map

  • ~40min

    Than Sadet Waterfall east coast

    Cascades and rock pools once visited by Thai kings, inside the national park. Steep road; go in the morning with a full tank. Map

  • ~35min

    Mae Haad & Koh Ma north-west

    The best snorkelling on the island. At low tide you can walk the sandbar to the little island; snorkel at high tide. Map

Full Moon Party

Once a month at Haad Rin, about 25 minutes away. The party stays on that beach — parties at the villa are not allowed. Taxis at night are scarce and expensive, so sort out your ride first, and please don't ride a scooter after drinking. Fluorescent paint doesn't wash out of sheets or towels: rinse it off before you come inside.

Boat trips

  • ~8hours

    Koh Tao & Nang Yuan

    A full snorkelling day with lunch. Boats leave from Thong Sala, five minutes from the villa.
