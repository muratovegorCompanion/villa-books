The house

A few things that work differently from home.

Pool

Covered, on its own level, open day and night all year. It's only lightly chlorinated. Please keep an eye on children — at the pool and on the inside stairs.

Air conditioning

Five units: every bedroom plus the living areas. Please switch them off when you go out.

Towels

On every bed: two towels for the shower and two for the pool.

Coffee & kitchen

A full kitchen and a Nespresso machine with capsules.

Drinking water

Don't drink from the tap — the house runs on tank water. Bottles are at the 7‑Eleven in Chaloklum.

Projector

Connects by HDMI cable only — bring a laptop or an adapter for your phone.

Power cuts

Chaloklum sometimes loses power for up to two hours. It's the island grid, not the house — it comes back on its own.

Sunrise

You won't see the sunset from the house, but the sunrise is worth an early alarm.

House rules

No parties or events — the neighbours are close and it's a quiet road. Smoking and vaping only on the terrace. Small dogs are welcome; for bigger animals, ask us first.

On the day you leave

  1. Be out by 11:00 — the cleaners come at 11.
  2. Close the windows and lock the door.
  3. Put the key back in the key box to the right of the door, or hand it to the cleaners if they're already here.

Getting around

There's no Grab or Bolt on Koh Phangan, and taxis are expensive. Most guests rent a scooter or a car — and get delivered to the door.

Our neighbours

Two Thai sisters, sign by the parking. Scooters delivered to the house; taxi too. Just message them when you need one — no booking ahead.

Telling a driver where to go

Say “Ao Hin Ngam beach, Chaloklum”. The house is on the same street: turn at the sign, it's on the right behind the bamboo fence with lanterns.

To the pier

A taxi from the house to Thong Sala pier is about 700 ฿ for three. Groups of six with luggage need a big taxi‑bus.

On a scooter

Wear a helmet — that's what the police check. The last climb to the house is steep but concrete; scooters and cars make it fine.

Parking

Free, right by the house, no steps up to the door.

Beaches

Times are from the house by scooter or car. Our own beach, Ao Hin Ngam, is 5 minutes on foot but rocky — lovely for a walk, not for swimming.

  • 2–5min

    Chaloklum & Malibu Beach north, the village

    Shallow and calm, the best for small kids. Malibu has the softest sand on this side, and there's usually a volleyball game going on.

  • 5min + stairs

    Haad Khom · Coral Bay

    A tiny cove with a reef right off the beach — snorkelling from the sand. Rocky at low tide.

  • 10–15min

    Mae Haad & Koh Ma our favourite

    The best snorkelling on the island. At low tide you can walk the sandbar to the little island; snorkel at high tide. Stay for the sunset.

  • ~20min

    Zen Beach · Sri Thanu Friday sunset

    Every Friday people from all over the island gather on the sand for a drum jam — drums, guitars, dancing, sometimes fire shows. Come by 17:30 for a spot and parking; the sun goes down around 18:00. Grasshopper Beach Bar next to it has food and drinks. Calm, shallow water; watch for rocks and sea urchins.

  • ~20min

    Hin Kong · Wok Tum west coast

    Come for the sunset, not for a swim: the water is shallow, so it's good at high tide and a wide sandbank at low tide. Dinner nearby at L'Alcove or Sati Pot — see Eat.

  • 20–25min

    Haad Salad & Haad Yao west coast

    Easy, calm water, good with kids. Haad Yao has proper sunsets.

  • ~50min

    Haad Rin south-east

    A kilometre of fine white sand and clear water on the Sunrise side, with longtail boats pulled up on the beach. The other side of the headland, Haad Rin Nai, faces the sunset.

Views & walks

  • 5min

    Kuan Yin Chinese Temple

    Climb the steps for a view over the whole bay. 40 ฿, open 8:00–17:00.

  • walk

    From the village through the forest. Medium effort.

  • 15–20min

    Phaeng Waterfall & Domsila viewpoint

    An easy hike, 100 ฿, with a view to Koh Samui.

  • —

    Paradise Waterfall

    A jungle pool with a rope swing. Rough road and a steep last stretch; sometimes there's a party on.

    A hard climb — better with a guide.

Shopping

7‑Eleven with an ATM in Chaloklum; fruit and vegetables at the village stalls. The big supermarkets — Lotus's and Makro — are in Thong Sala, about 25 minutes. A fan, if you want one, is sold at Lotus's and Big C.

Further out

Beaches

  • 30min by boat

    Bottle Beach

    Longtail boat from Chaloklum pier (or a 1–2 hour jungle trail). Remote and quiet — just swimming and doing nothing.

  • 40min

    Thong Nai Pan Noi north-east

    One of the most beautiful bays on the island. The road is hilly — go by car if you're not confident on a scooter.

Full Moon Party

Once a month at Haad Rin, about 50 minutes away. The party stays on that beach — parties at the villa are still not allowed. Taxis home at night are scarce and expensive, so sort out your ride first, and please don't take a scooter after drinking. Fluorescent paint doesn't wash out of sheets or towels: rinse it off at the outdoor shower before you come inside.

Trips

  • fullday

    Ang Thong Marine Park

    Kayaks, snorkelling and the Emerald Lake, about 2,200 ฿ with lunch. Closed roughly from 1 November to mid‑December.

  • dayfrom Mae Haad

    Rope park on Koh Ma

    Onepen Adventure Park: a rope course with two ziplines, a chain bridge and a giant spider web. A day package with snorkelling is about 2,500 ฿ a person. Starts at 10:00 from Mae Haad.

  • ~8hours

    Koh Tao & Nang Yuan

    A full snorkelling day with lunch.

  • ½day

    Boat along the north coast

    Koh Ma, Haad Khom and Bottle Beach by longtail from Chaloklum.

  • dive

    Chaloklum Diving in the village

    Sail Rock, try‑dives, groups of up to four.

  • ~20min

    Yoga in Sri Thanu

    Samma Karuna or Orion Healing, 250–400 ฿ a class.
